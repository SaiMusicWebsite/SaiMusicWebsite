About Me

Hey! I'm Sai Abhyankkar 👋

Sai Abhyankkar is a talented Indian music composer, playback singer, music producer and multi-instrumentalist known for bringing a fresh and energetic sound to Tamil music.

He gained widespread recognition with his independent hit "Katchi Sera", followed by popular songs such as "Aasa Kooda" and "Pavazha Malli". His music blends contemporary sounds with elements of Tamil musical culture, creating a unique identity of his own.
